About Alex

I co-designed a cookstove that hit 40% thermal efficiency for $30 a unit for communities in Senegal and Sierra Leone. That project, with Burn Design Lab, is a decent snapshot of how I like to work: practical and focused on what actually gets built and used to support the environment.

I've applied that same approach across some pretty different fields: replacing a $25,000 acoustic release mechanism with a sub-$5,000 servo-actuated version for a river turbine in Alaska, and prototyping organ perfusion hardware for kidney vitrification research.

Right now, I'm a master's student at Columbia, focused on energy systems.

Outside of engineering, I spend my time in nature. I spend my weekends hiking, camping, and cycling (I bikepacked over 1,000 kms across Europe last summer)!

I'm looking for an internship for Summer 2027. Whether you're looking for engineers or just want to connect, please reach out.
