About Alex

I am a mechanical engineering master's student at Columbia University, focused on energy systems, sustainable product development, and physical prototyping. My work sits between analysis and fabrication: I use CAD, testing, and iteration to turn difficult physical problems into products and systems that can be built and improved. Recent projects include clean cookstoves for communities in Senegal and Sierra Leone, hydrokinetic river-turbine hardware for Alaska, and an organ perfusion system for donor-kidney research.
